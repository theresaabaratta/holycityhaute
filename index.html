About HCH

The Vision

Garden dinner parties, beachside brunches, a disco night with the whole bridal party on the dance floor — Holy City Haute designs, builds and runs the celebration around the host. Bring your own idea and we shape it, or borrow ours and let the HCH look lead the way.

How HCH Works

01

You're hosting, and you want the event to look effortless and feel elevated — in a word, haute.

02

Send us your date and a few details. We set up a consult to learn the bride, the group and the mood, then build a plan around it.

03

We source, style and set up everything — linens, florals, tableware, signage and favors — so the space is ready when your group walks in.

04

Breakdown and cleanup are on us. You stay in the moment with your people.

More Charleston Party Planning

Once the tables are set, your group still needs a full weekend plan. Party Charleston covers nightlife, bachelorette activities and things to do downtown, while Explore Charleston is the official visitor guide for neighborhoods, history and events. For what's new around town, Charleston City Paper keeps up with restaurants, bars and weekend happenings.

Getting out on the water is a must for most bach groups. Browse private charters on GetMyBoat, or book a shared sunset cruise out of Shem Creek through Batch. For more ideas built around the bride, Charleston Bachelorette Activities rounds up boat days, tours and group outings, and A Paige of Positivity shares a real Charleston bach itinerary with beach picnic and Folly Beach picks.

Hosting a bigger dinner or shower? Curated Events rents linens, tabletop and dance floors for social events, and boutique rental house CHEERS focuses on unique, luxe pieces for dinner parties. EventHaus and Charleston Tents & Events cover tents, lighting and seating for outdoor setups, and Mount Pleasant florist CC Bloom Florals handles custom arrangements.

For styling inspiration, Charleston Weddings Magazine features local tablescapes and gal-pal weekend shoots, and Wed Society Charleston profiles specialty linen and rental companies. When you're ready to book group experiences, Bach Events CHS keeps them in one place, and A Charleston Party walks through party types and décor from start to finish.
